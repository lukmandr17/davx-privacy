Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What the app accesses

With your permission, DavX LDR uses the Google People API (contacts scope) to read and update your Google Contacts, and reads and writes the contacts stored on your phone, only to keep the two in sync. It also reads your Google account email address to show which account is signed in.

Where your data goes

  • Contact data travels only between your phone and Google's servers. DavX LDR has no server of its own and does not send your data anywhere else.
  • Sign-in tokens and sync state are stored on your phone, encrypted with Android Keystore.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Limited Use

DavX LDR's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use “Logout” in the app to revoke its access, or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes its local data.

Ringkasan (Bahasa Indonesia)

DavX LDR hanya membaca dan menulis kontak Google-mu untuk disinkronkan dengan kontak di HP-mu sendiri. Tidak ada server milik app, tidak ada iklan atau pelacakan, dan data tidak dibagikan ke pihak lain. Token login disimpan terenkripsi di HP.
